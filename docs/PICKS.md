## 3. Projection divergence — model vs market

*Calibration record, NOT bets.*

> Divergence means our number disagrees with the market — it does **not** mean the market is wrong. The closing price already contains every sharp model working on this game; when we disagree, the more likely explanation is that our number is worse. Until this board beats its baseline, read a large divergence as a warning about our projection, not an opportunity.
